# Photos for the website

The site is designed to work with or without photos. Every image slot has a
warm illustrated fallback, so nothing looks broken while you collect pictures.
To bring in the real Instagram photos, save them here with these exact names.
Landscape or square JPGs around 1600px wide are ideal.

| File | Where it shows | Suggested shot |
|---|---|---|
| `hero.jpg` | Full-screen hero behind the headline | Wide shot of the restaurant at golden hour, or the sea from the terrace |
| `chef.jpg` | "The chef" section, portrait frame (4:5) | The chef at the grill or at a table, smiling |
| `dish-steak-mash.jpg` | Guest favourites card | Steak and mash, close up |
| `dish-almond-steak.jpg` | Guest favourites card | Almond steak |
| `dish-fajitas.jpg` | Guest favourites card | Sizzling chicken fajitas |
| `dish-seafood.jpg` | Guest favourites card | Grilled fish or seafood |
| `dish-pasta.jpg` | Guest favourites card | Pasta dish |
| `dish-dessert.jpg` | Guest favourites card | Any dessert |
| `ig-1.jpg` … `ig-6.jpg` | Instagram strip (square tiles) | Six recent posts |

To change which dishes appear, edit the cards in `index.html` under the
`<!-- MENU -->` comment. Each card is one `<article class="dish">` block.
