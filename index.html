The chef

He spent years in professional kitchens. Then he came home to cook the way his grandfather did.

Grandpa's Kitchen started with a simple idea: food should taste like someone cared. After a career as a working chef, the owner came back to Dahab to open the restaurant he had always dreamed about. Small enough to know your name, serious enough to rest a steak properly.

Every sauce is made in-house. The mash is whipped to order. The playlist is his. Most nights you'll find him somewhere between the grill and your table, asking how it was.

  • 🔥 From scratch No shortcuts, no packets. Stocks, sauces and marinades are made here, daily.
  • 👨‍🍳 Chef-owned One kitchen, one cook who answers for every plate that leaves it.
  • 🌅 Dahab slow Nothing is rushed. Order, breathe, watch the light change on the mountains.

The town

Dahab means gold. You'll understand at sunset.

A small Bedouin town squeezed between the Sinai mountains and the Red Sea, where the day runs on dive schedules and the evening runs on nothing at all.

The Blue Hole

One of the most famous dive sites on earth, a 15-minute drive north. Snorkel the rim, or go deep with a guide.

The Lighthouse

Reef straight off the shore in the heart of town. Learn to dive here in the morning, eat here in the afternoon.

The Lagoon

Shallow turquoise water and steady wind. Kitesurfers, windsurfers and anyone who just wants to float.

The Mountains

Granite peaks that turn pink, then gold, then purple. Bedouin tea in a wadi is the best dessert in Sinai.

Dahab time

Nobody is in a hurry. Plans change with the wind. The only fixed appointment is sunset, and it never cancels.

Good to know before you come

Practical notes for first-timers, gathered from the people who make the trip every year.

Getting here

Dahab has no airport. Fly to Sharm El Sheikh, about 90 km and an hour and a half south, then take a taxi or the bus up the coast road.

When to visit

Spring and autumn are the sweet spot. Summer runs hot at 30 to 37°C with the warmest sea. Winter is quiet, cheaper, and the water drops to around 21 to 24°C, so bring a thicker wetsuit.

Snorkel from town

The Lighthouse, Eel Garden and the Canyon are all reachable from the shore. Eel Garden is a sandy plain where hundreds of garden eels sway in the current.

Day trips

The Blue Hole and Ras Abu Galum sit 25 minutes north. Mount Sinai and St Catherine's Monastery make a classic overnight: climb through the night, watch sunrise from the summit.

Where we are

El Melil Street, a short walk from the Masbat seafront promenade and the Lighthouse reef. Dive in the morning, eat with us from 1pm.

Bedouin hospitality

Dahab was a Bedouin fishing village before it was anything else. Say yes to the tea. The Bedouin community still shapes the rhythm of the town.
